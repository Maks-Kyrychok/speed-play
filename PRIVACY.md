# Privacy Policy for SpeedyPlay

_Last updated: 17 September 2026_

SpeedyPlay does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## What is stored

The extension stores two settings — your selected playback speed and whether it
should be applied automatically. They are kept in `chrome.storage.local`, which
lives on your own computer. They are never uploaded anywhere, and they are
removed when you uninstall the extension.

## What is not collected

SpeedyPlay does not collect browsing history, watched videos, search queries,
account information, IP addresses, or analytics of any kind. It contains no
trackers and makes no network requests.

## Permissions

- **storage** — saves your speed settings on your device.
- **Access to www.youtube.com** — lets the extension add the speed button to
  the player and change playback speed. It runs on no other site, including
  YouTube Music and the mobile site.
